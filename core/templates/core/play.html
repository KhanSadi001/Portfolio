How binary works

Each bit is worth double the one to its right: 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32, 64, 128. Turn on the bits whose values add up to the target. 42 is 32 + 8 + 2, so it's 00101010.

Hex packs 4 bits into one digit (0–9, then A–F). 0x2A is 2 × 16 + 10 = 42.
